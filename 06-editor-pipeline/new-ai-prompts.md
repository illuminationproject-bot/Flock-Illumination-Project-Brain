# New AI Prompts for the Live → Waterfall Pipeline

> These two prompts don't exist in the Notion AI Prompt Library yet — copy them in. They power
> Stage 1 (Edit Map) and Stage 2/3 (packaging research) of `live-to-waterfall-sop.md`.

## Edit Map prompt (Stage 1)

Use when: you have a full transcript with timestamps from a live/recording.

```
Here is the full timestamped transcript of a recording. Produce an Edit Map with six sections:

1. THESIS — the one idea this whole recording argues for, in one sentence.
2. CHAPTERS — natural topic breaks with timestamp ranges and a one-line summary each.
3. KEEPER MOMENTS — quotable lines, story beats, and energy peaks, each with timestamp and
   one sentence on why it's gold.
4. HOOK CANDIDATES — the 5 strongest cold-open moments (timestamp + the exact spoken line).
5. CUT LIST — tangents, restarts, technical issues, and sections that add no value
   (timestamp ranges + reason).
6. CAPTION FLAGS — names, faith/worship vocabulary, song titles, and any term an
   auto-captioner will likely get wrong, with correct spellings.

Audience: 25–35 year old worship pastors and Christian creatives. Be decisive — every
section of the transcript ends up either in a chapter, a keeper moment, or the cut list.

Transcript: [PASTE]
```

## Title + packaging research prompt (Stages 2–3)

Use when: you have the thesis/chapter summary and your 1of10 + vidIQ findings.

```
I'm packaging a YouTube video for an audience of worship pastors and Christian creatives
(25–35). Here is the video's core idea, plus title patterns from outlier videos in our niche
(from 1of10) and keyword data (from vidIQ).

Write 3 title candidates. Rules: under 60 characters; name the insight or tension, not the
topic; the title must be a promise the video literally fulfills; no clickbait gap, no
em-dashes, don't start with "How". For each title, add: (a) which outlier pattern it uses,
(b) a 3–5 word thumbnail text, and (c) one sentence describing the thumbnail image concept
(one focal point, visible emotion).

Core idea: [PASTE]
Outlier title patterns: [PASTE FROM 1OF10]
Keyword notes: [PASTE FROM VIDIQ]
```
