# Master Content Calendar Plan

Two brands, five surfaces (YouTube long-form, YouTube Shorts, IG Reels, IG Feed, IG Stories), one production pipeline. Everything is made once and adapted per platform — never posted identically everywhere.

## Content pillars

| # | Pillar | What it is | Primary CTA |
|---|--------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Music & Performance** | Song clips, live moments, studio takes, releases | Listen / subscribe |
| 2 | **Message & Teaching** | The meaning behind the music, mini-lessons, spoken word | Save / share / join Flock |
| 3 | **Behind the Scenes** | Process, gear, writing sessions, real life of the project | Follow / comment |
| 4 | **Community (Flock)** | Member spotlights, UGC, Skool wins, inside-the-community moments | Join the Flock (Skool) |
| 5 | **Personal Story & Vision** | Origin story, struggles, why this exists | Comment / DM keyword |
| 6 | **Invitation** | Direct offers: join Skool, new release, event, livestream | Link in bio / DM keyword |

Rough weekly mix: 30% pillar 1, 25% pillar 2, 15% pillar 3, 15% pillar 4, 10% pillar 5, 5% pillar 6. The Illumination Project page leans pillars 1–3 + 5; the Flock page leans pillars 2, 4, 6.

At the 2-reels/day pace: reel #1 follows the day's theme, reel #2 is a repurposed cut from the week's long-form video (this is how 1 YouTube video becomes 4–6 shorts).

## Platform adaptation rules

**One asset, five outputs.** Every reel is produced once in 9:16, then:

- **Instagram Reels** — hook text on screen in first 1.5s, native captions, 3–5 niche hashtags, CTA in caption + pinned comment. Trending or original audio.
- **YouTube Shorts** — same video, but title written for search ("How I wrote…", "Why…"), `#Shorts` in title/description, end-screen verbal CTA to the long-form video or channel.
- **IG Stories** — reels get reshared to stories with an interactive sticker (poll, slider, question, link). Never a bare reshare.
- **IG Feed post/carousel** — 4:5 crop. Carousels: slide 1 = hook, middle slides = value, last slide = CTA. Caption written as a mini-essay, not a repeat of the slides.
- **YouTube long-form** — 8+ min. Thumbnail + title A/B thought through before filming. Chapters in description. Verbal CTA at the point of highest retention (~30–60s in), not just at the end.

**Cross-brand rule:** a reel goes on *both* pages only if it serves both audiences (rare). Default: each brand gets its own native reel daily. Flock content can reference the Illumination Project; the Illumination Project funnels toward Flock in CTAs.

## Weekly production batch (one shoot day feeds the whole week)

1. **Film day (pick one, e.g., Wednesday):** 2–3 hrs — film 7–10 reel concepts + 1–2 long-form videos + b-roll for stories.
2. **Edit block:** cut reels, pull 3–5 shorts from each long-form video, design carousels/graphics.
3. **Copy block:** captions, titles, hashtags, story text written in one sitting from the idea log (`05-ideas/`).
4. **Sunday:** full week delivered for approval (see `02-ghl/approval-workflow.md`).
5. **Monday:** approved content scheduled in GHL (see `02-ghl/ghl-social-planner-setup.md` and `ghl-bulk-upload-template.csv`).
