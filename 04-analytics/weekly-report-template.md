## KPI north stars (review monthly)

1. **Skool joins per week** — the business metric everything feeds.
2. **Reel/Short average view duration** — the leading indicator of reach growth.
3. **Long-form watch time** — the depth metric that builds true fans.

Vanity metrics (follower count alone) never drive decisions; funnel metrics do.
