# Content Ideas — How the Daily Idea Engine Works

3 fresh content ideas are logged every day in `05-ideas/log/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, each tagged with pillar, format, brand, and a written hook — so film days pull from a stocked backlog instead of a blank page.

## Idea format

```md
## Idea N — {working title}
- Pillar: {1–6}
- Brand: IP / Flock / both
- Format: reel+short / carousel / long-form / story arc
- Hook (first line on screen): "..."
- Beat sheet: 2–4 bullets of what happens
- CTA: ...
```

## Evergreen idea wells (when in doubt, pull from these)

1. **Answer a real question** someone asked in Skool/DMs/comments this week.
2. **Break down one lyric** — what it means, what inspired it.
3. **Show the unfinished thing** — voice memo → finished song, sketch → cover art.
4. **React/duet** a fan cover, comment, or member win.
5. **Contrarian take** on the music industry / spiritual scene, said with love.
6. **Day-one story** — what was true when this started that people don't know.
7. **Process timelapse** with a teaching voiceover on top.
8. **"POV" format** tied to the feeling a song resolves.
9. **Live moment** — crowd, soundcheck, prayer/meditation before a set.
10. **Weekly Flock recap** — 3 things that happened inside the community.
